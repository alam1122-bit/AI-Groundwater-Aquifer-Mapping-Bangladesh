# AI-Powered Multi-Layer Groundwater and Aquifer Mapping in Bangladesh

## Summary
This project proposes an AI-based system to estimate possible groundwater aquifer depths at selected locations in Bangladesh. The goal is to explore how geological, hydrogeological, and geographical data can help identify potential groundwater-bearing layers and support better-informed drilling decisions.

## Background
Groundwater is an important source of drinking water and irrigation in Bangladesh. However, identifying suitable aquifers and assessing groundwater quality can be challenging. An AI-based mapping system could help researchers explore groundwater patterns and estimate potential aquifer depths. The project will also investigate whether available data can support predictions of groundwater-quality indicators.

## Data and AI Techniques
- Relevant datasets will be identified and collected from available sources, including geological maps, borehole records, groundwater measurements, and laboratory water-quality records.
- GIS and satellite-derived data may be explored as additional features.
- Depending on data availability, machine learning techniques such as Random Forest and regression models may be used to estimate aquifer depths or groundwater-quality indicators.

## How Is It Used?
The proposed system will allow users to select a location on a map or enter GPS coordinates. Based on available data, it will estimate possible aquifer depths and, where sufficient data exist, predict selected groundwater-quality indicators. The results will be presented as estimates rather than confirmed measurements. Field investigation and laboratory testing will still be necessary before making drilling or drinking-water decisions.

## Challenges
- The main challenges are finding reliable and sufficiently detailed datasets, dealing with missing information, and accounting for geographical variations in groundwater conditions. 
- Model accuracy will depend on the quality and quantity of available data. Predictions alone cannot guarantee that groundwater at a particular depth is safe for drinking.

## What Next?
The first step will be to investigate data availability for a selected region of Bangladesh, potentially around the Kushiara River in Moulvibazar. After identifying suitable datasets, the project will focus on data preparation, model development, and evaluation. A simple map-based prototype may then be developed to display estimated aquifer depths and relevant predictions.
